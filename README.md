

# 🦠 COVID-19 Pandemic Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This dashboard tracks the global spread of COVID-19 using confirmed cases, deaths, and recoveries from the **Johns Hopkins University** dataset. It highlights total impact, recovery trends, and the most affected countries through interactive charts and filters, built using **Power BI**.

**Internship:** Analyst Lab Africa

---

## 🛠️ Tool Used
- Power BI — Data modeling, querying & visualization

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview
- Total Confirmed Cases — 1bn
- Total Deaths — 39M
- Recovery Rate — 0.26
- Active Cases — 828M

---

## 💡 Key Findings
- 📈 **Confirmed cases rose sharply from mid-2020**, climbing steeply after July 2020 with no signs of slowing
- 🇬🇧 **United Kingdom recorded the highest confirmed cases** at 0.56bn, followed by France at 0.39bn and China at 0.12bn
- 📉 **Recovery rates declined steadily over time**, dropping from near 100% in early 2020 to below 20% by January 2021
- 🌍 **Canada and China led in total recoveries** by country/region, with a sharp drop-off among remaining countries
- ☠️ Countries like **Algeria, Argentina, and Australia** recorded notably high death tolls among the dataset's regions
